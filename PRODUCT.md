# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Decision-makers at small and mid-size businesses (clinic owners, barbershop chains, distributors, sales teams, founders) evaluating whether to hire Pedro Tortoriello to build software, apply AI, or automate their operations. They arrive skeptical, short on time, and comparing him against agencies and other freelancers. They need to feel, within seconds, that this person ships real systems and understands their business, not just code.

The job to be done: judge competence and fit fast, then start a conversation (WhatsApp).

## Product Purpose

A conversion-focused personal site that repositions Pedro from "full stack developer" to **AI & automation specialist who builds SaaS and systems that scale businesses**. Success = a qualified visitor books a conversation. Every section exists to move skepticism toward a WhatsApp message: clear positioning, proof through real shipped products, a legible working method, and an honest results narrative.

## Brand Personality

Authority, technical confidence, calm precision. Voice is direct and specific (numbers, named products, real outcomes), never hype. Three words: **engineered, assured, modern.** It should feel like a senior operator's studio, not a template portfolio. Emotional goal: "this person clearly ships, and clearly gets the business side."

## Anti-references

- The generic dark "AI startup" template: blue→purple gradient text, glassmorphism on every card, the hero-metric cliché, a tracked uppercase eyebrow above every section.
- The generic developer portfolio (skill bars, "Hi I'm X", emoji, timeline of bootcamps).
- Loud, over-animated agency sites where motion is decoration, not meaning.

## Design Principles

1. **Proof over claims.** Lead with real shipped products and concrete outcomes; let the work carry the credibility (inherited from the korbrasil reference's results-driven stance).
2. **Restraint reads as senior.** Match the discipline of Linear/Vercel/Stripe: one signature color, precise type, surgical depth. Effects appear only where they earn it.
3. **Business-first language.** Frame every technical capability as a business result (a system that operates itself, a pipeline that fills, an operation under control).
4. **Motion with intent.** Orchestrated entrance and scroll reveals that fit what they reveal; never a uniform fade on every block. Always a reduced-motion path.
5. **Fast and legible.** Mobile-first, Lighthouse 95+, AA contrast. Premium is also performance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 on the dark base, large text ≥3:1. Full keyboard navigation and visible focus rings. `prefers-reduced-motion` honored on every animation (crossfade/instant fallback). Portuguese (pt-BR) primary.
